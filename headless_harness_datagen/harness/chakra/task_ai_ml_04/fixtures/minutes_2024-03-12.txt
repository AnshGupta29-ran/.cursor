CITY OF RIVERBEND — Planning Commission Minutes
Meeting Date: 2024-03-12

Chair Lopez called the meeting to order at 6:02 p.m. Roll call: Lopez, Nguyen, Ortiz, Patel present; Reed absent.

Public comment: two speakers opposed unrestricted short-term rentals near the pier; one speaker supported income for ADU owners.

MOTION by Nguyen, second by Ortiz: recommend City Council adopt Ordinance 2024-07 Short-Term Rental Cap with a 90-day waitlist and owner-occupancy requirement for new registrations.

ROLL-CALL VOTE on the motion:
- Lopez: Aye
- Nguyen: Aye
- Ortiz: Aye
- Patel: Nay
Motion carried 3–1. Findings cite neighborhood petition (42 signatures) and fire marshal egress memo.

Adjourned 7:41 p.m. Minutes prepared by Clerk Rivera.
