ORDINANCE 2024-07 — Short-Term Rental Cap (excerpt)
City of Riverbend

Section 1. Purpose. Regulate short-term rentals to protect housing stock and public safety near the waterfront.

Section 2. Cap. New short-term rental registrations shall enter a 90-day waitlist when active licenses exceed the council-set neighborhood cap.

Section 3. Owner-occupancy. Applicants for new registrations must certify primary residence on the parcel for at least 270 days per calendar year.

Section 4. Enforcement. Violations may result in license suspension after notice and a public hearing before the hearing examiner.

Section 5. Effective date. This ordinance takes effect 30 days after City Council adoption.
