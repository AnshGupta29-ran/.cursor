# Attribution

Tidewatch ships with **no third-party or licensed assets**.

- **Art:** all visuals are generated at runtime from Unity primitive meshes (cubes, spheres,
  quads, cylinders) with solid-color `Standard` materials. No textures, models, or sprites
  are imported.
- **Audio:** all sound effects and the music bed / ocean ambience are **procedurally
  synthesized** at startup in `AudioManager.cs` (tone, noise, and filtered-noise generation).
  No audio files are imported.
- **Fonts:** UI uses Unity's built-in runtime font (`LegacyRuntime.ttf`).

Everything is original and generated in code; there is nothing to license and nothing to
attribute beyond the Unity engine itself.

- **Engine:** [Unity](https://unity.com/) 2022.3 LTS (© Unity Technologies). Unity is
  required to open, run, and build the project and is not redistributed.
